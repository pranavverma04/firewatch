NASA FIRMS on its own reaches us about three hours after a pass. FireWatch now reads five polar orbiters and two geostationary satellites side by side, checks the cloud over the point, and turns every answer into one number an officer can defend.

Pick a point

Each satellite is asked on its own. A yes multiplies the odds by its weight; a clear-sky no lowers them a little; a no under cloud counts for nothing.

Method

The weights, in the open

The three FireWatch rules still set the risk tier. This check answers a different question: is the heat real and is it still there? It starts at 35% for one raw alert and multiplies the odds by each weight below. It is capped at 97%, because no number of satellites replaces a ground visit.
